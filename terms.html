Scorecard Terms

LinkedIn Buyer Confusion Scorecard · Brenda Blanche™

These terms explain what the Scorecard is, what it isn't, and what you can expect from using it.

What this is

The LinkedIn Buyer Confusion Scorecard is a self-assessment tool. You answer questions about how your LinkedIn presence currently communicates who you help, what problem you solve, what changes for a buyer, what proof you offer, and what action you invite. Based on your own answers, the tool calculates a Buyer Clarity Score and generates a personalized result.

What this is not

This is not:

  • An objective market study or independent research finding
  • A guarantee of inquiries, clients, or revenue
  • A measure of your talent, intelligence, or professional worth
  • A substitute for legal, financial, or professional advice
  • A complete positioning, proof, or offer strategy on its own

Your result is directional — it reflects the information and self-reported signals you provide, and is intended to help you prioritize what to look at first, not to make claims about outcomes.

Accuracy of your answers

Your result is only as useful as the honesty and accuracy of what you enter. Deliberately inaccurate answers won't produce a meaningful diagnosis.

Recommended next steps

Depending on your result, the Scorecard may recommend either the Expertise-to-Recognition Playbook or a Perception Call with Brenda. These are optional. Completing the Scorecard does not obligate you to purchase anything, and your result remains yours to use however you'd like, with or without further engagement.

Intellectual property

The Scorecard's questions, scoring methodology, and diagnostic framework are the property of Brenda Blanche. You're welcome to use your own result for your own purposes, but the tool itself, its underlying logic, and its written content may not be copied, reproduced, or redistributed without permission.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time as the tool evolves. The version in effect at the time you complete the Scorecard is the one that applies to your submission.
