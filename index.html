You know how to do the work. Selling it is the hard part.

You may even have years of experience, results you’re proud of, and knowledge that could genuinely help someone. But turning all of that into an offer people understand, value, and want to pay for? That’s where things can get frustrating.

Maybe you can do five different things well, but you’re not sure which one to build your business around.
Maybe people look at your profile and still don’t understand exactly what they should hire you for.
Maybe you keep creating content, explaining your expertise, and showing up online, but it doesn’t seem to translate into the kind of buying conversations you’re hoping for.
Or maybe you know your work is valuable, but when someone asks what you do, you struggle to explain it in a way that makes them immediately understand why it matters to them.

You’re good at the work. But you’re not sure the market sees its value as clearly as you do.

Find out what’s getting in the way

Six areas that decide how well your expertise turns into business.

The Market Perception Scorecard helps you examine six areas that influence how effectively your expertise translates into a business people can understand and consider buying from. In about five minutes, you’ll assess:

01Market & Problem Clarity

Are you solving a problem your intended buyers actually recognize and care about?

02Offer Design

Have you turned your skills into something specific that people can understand and buy?

03Positioning & Differentiation

Is it clear why your work matters and why your approach is worth considering?

04Evidence & Trust

Can potential buyers find reasons to believe you can deliver what you say you can?

05Market Signals & Recognition

Do your profile, content, and messaging reinforce what you want to be known for?

06Buyer Journey & Conversion

Is there a clear next step for someone who becomes interested in working with you?
