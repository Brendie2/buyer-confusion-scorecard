The Problem

Expertise is not automatically legible to the market.

You can be highly capable, experienced, and deeply committed to your work while still being difficult for the right buyer to place, trust, or choose.

"I have many skills"→"This is the problem I solve."
"I have experience"→"Here is evidence a buyer can understand."
"I can help in many ways"→"This is the next action available to you."

Fit Check

Who this is for — and who it isn't.

For You If
  • You are capable but difficult for the market to place.
  • Your LinkedIn presence doesn't clearly communicate your commercial value.
  • You have several skills but need a more coherent market-facing direction.
  • Your offer exists but is hard to explain or choose.
  • You have proof but don't know how to use it ethically.
  • Your content is active but your next action is unclear.
Not Designed For
  • People looking for a popularity or virality score.
  • People seeking a guaranteed revenue prediction.
  • People expecting a diagnostic to replace strategic judgment.
  • People wanting fabricated testimonials or exaggerated claims.
  • People whose immediate need is specialized legal, medical, or financial advice.
Questions

Before you start.

How long does the Scorecard take?

About 5–7 minutes for 20 scored statements plus a few context questions. There's no way to save and return mid-way, so it's easiest to complete in one sitting.

Is this a popularity or LinkedIn performance score?

No. It has nothing to do with followers, likes, or reach. It measures whether a buyer can understand who you help, what problem you solve, and what to do next — based on your own honest assessment of your profile.

Does the Scorecard predict revenue?

No. It's a directional diagnostic, not a guarantee of inquiries, clients, or revenue. It identifies where your message may be creating uncertainty and gives you a specific next step.

What happens after I receive my result?

You'll see your Buyer Clarity Score, your primary gap, and a recommended route based on your specific situation — that might be a self-directed correction, or a conversation with Brenda if your case is more complex.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your answers are used only to calculate and deliver your result and, if you opt in, for relevant follow-up. You control what you consent to on the final screen before seeing your result.

What if my result doesn't feel accurate?

That's useful information too. Your self-assessment is compared against your reported real-world signals — if the two conflict, the result will say so rather than pretending the score is the whole picture.
