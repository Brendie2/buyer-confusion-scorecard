Privacy Policy

LinkedIn Buyer Confusion Scorecard · Brenda Blanche™

This page explains what information the LinkedIn Buyer Confusion Scorecard collects, why it's collected, how it's used, and what choices you have.

What we collect

When you complete the Scorecard, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your WhatsApp number, if you choose to provide it
  • Your answers to the diagnostic questions, qualification questions, and reported market signal questions
  • Your consent choices (see below)

We do not ask for payment details, passwords, or any sensitive personal information, and you should avoid including confidential client information in any open-text answer.

Why we collect it

Your information is used to:

  • Calculate your Buyer Clarity Score and generate your personalized result
  • Email you that result
  • Notify Brenda so she has context for any follow-up conversation you choose to have
  • Where you've opted in, send you relevant guidance or contact you on WhatsApp

Where it's stored

Your submission is processed through Cloudflare, delivered by email via Resend, and stored in a private Airtable database that only Brenda has access to. None of these services are permitted to sell your data, and none of your answers are made public.

Your consent choices

The Scorecard separates three kinds of consent, and only one is required to see your result:

  • Required: processing your answers to calculate and deliver your result
  • Optional: receiving ongoing LinkedIn and market-perception guidance by email
  • Optional: being contacted or sent your result via WhatsApp

Declining the optional consents does not affect your ability to complete the Scorecard or receive your result.

How to request deletion

You can ask to have your submission deleted at any time by contacting Brenda directly (see below). We'll remove your record from Airtable and confirm once it's done.

Third-party processors

This tool relies on a small number of trusted services to function: Cloudflare (hosting), Resend (email delivery), and Airtable (secure storage of your submission). Each processes your data only as needed to perform that specific function.

Questions about your data? Contact Brenda directly at unapologeticquenn@gmail.com.
